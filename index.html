01 / NERVOUS SYSTEM

DETECT. PROCESS. RESPOND.

The nervous system helps organisms coordinate their activities and respond to changes in their environment.

👁️
01

Receptor

Detects stimuli such as light, heat, sound and pressure.

⚡
02

Neuron

Transmits electrical impulses through the nervous system.

💪
03

Effector

Muscles or glands produce the required response.

02 / COMMAND CENTRE

THE BRAIN

The brain is the main coordinating centre of the nervous system.

FOREBRAIN

Thinking, memory, intelligence and sensory interpretation.

MIDBRAIN

Helps coordinate certain visual and auditory responses.

HINDBRAIN

Helps maintain balance, posture and involuntary activities.
