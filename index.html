NEURAL CORE

THE HUMAN BRAIN

The brain is the main coordinating centre of the nervous system. It receives information, processes it and sends instructions to different parts of the body.

🧠 NEURAL COMMAND CENTRE
🧠

Cerebrum

Controls thinking, memory, intelligence, reasoning and voluntary actions.

⚖️

Cerebellum

Helps maintain balance, posture and coordination of muscular movements.

⚡

Medulla

Controls involuntary activities such as breathing and heartbeat.

SIGNAL UNIT

THE NEURON

A neuron is the structural and functional unit of the nervous system.

NEURAL SIGNAL

HOW A NEURON WORKS

Dendrites: Receive information from receptors or other neurons.

Cell body: Processes information received by the neuron.

Axon: Carries the nerve impulse towards the nerve ending.

Synapse: Allows the signal to pass to another neuron.
