01 / THE BIG IDEA

DETECT. PROCESS. RESPOND.

Control and coordination helps organisms detect changes in their surroundings, process information and produce suitable responses.

👁️
01

DETECT

Receptors detect changes such as light, heat, sound, pressure and touch.

🧠
02

PROCESS

The nervous system receives and processes information.

💪
03

RESPOND

Muscles or glands carry out the appropriate response.

02 / COMMAND CENTRE

THE BRAIN.

The brain is the main coordinating centre of the nervous system. It receives information, interprets it and controls responses.

FOREBRAIN

Thinking, memory, intelligence and interpretation of sensory information.

MIDBRAIN

Helps coordinate certain visual and auditory responses.

HIND BRAIN

Helps control balance, posture and important involuntary activities.

07 / HORMONAL CONTROL

CHEMICAL MESSENGERS.

Hormones are chemical messengers produced by endocrine glands and transported through blood.

⚡

ADRENALINE

Helps prepare the body for rapid action during stressful situations.

🦋

THYROXINE

Helps regulate metabolism and requires iodine for its production.

🍬

INSULIN

Helps regulate the glucose level in the blood.
