01 / NERVOUS SYSTEM

DETECT. PROCESS. RESPOND.

The nervous system helps organisms coordinate their activities and respond to changes in their environment.

👁️
01

Receptor

Detects stimuli such as light, heat, sound, pressure and touch.

⚡

Neuron

A specialised cell that transmits electrical impulses.

💪
03

Effector

Muscles or glands that produce the required response.

02 / COMMAND CENTRE

THE BRAIN

The brain is the main coordinating centre of the nervous system.

FOREBRAIN

Thinking, memory, intelligence and interpretation of sensory information.

MIDBRAIN

Coordinates certain visual and auditory responses.

HIND BRAIN

Helps control balance, posture and important involuntary activities.

05 / NEURAL CONNECTION

THE SYNAPSE.

The junction between two neurons is called a synapse. Chemical messengers help transfer the signal across the tiny gap.

STEP 01

Electrical impulse reaches the end of the first neuron.

STEP 02

Chemical messengers are released into the synaptic gap.

STEP 03

The next neuron receives the signal.

🔗

NEURON → SYNAPSE → NEURON

08 / HORMONAL CONTROL

MESSAGES THROUGH HORMONES.

Hormones are chemical messengers produced by endocrine glands and transported through blood.

⚡

Adrenaline

Helps prepare the body for situations requiring quick action.

🦋

Thyroxine

Helps regulate metabolism and requires iodine for its production.

🍬

Insulin

Helps regulate the level of glucose in the blood.

09 / PLANT HORMONES

PLANTS HAVE THEIR OWN SIGNALS.

Plants coordinate many responses using chemical substances called plant hormones.

🌱
AUXIN

Growth

Auxin promotes cell growth and helps coordinate responses such as bending towards light.

🍎
ETHYLENE

Fruit Ripening

Ethylene is involved in processes including fruit ripening.

🌿
GIBBERELLINS

Plant Growth

Gibberellins promote growth and can help in stem elongation.

10 / PLANT MOVEMENTS

PLANTS IN MOTION.

☀️
PHOTOTROPISM

Light

Shoots generally grow towards light.

🌍
GEOTROPISM

Gravity

Roots generally grow towards gravity, while shoots generally grow away from it.

💧
HYDROTROPISM

Water

Roots show growth towards a source of water.

🌸
CHEMOTROPISM

Chemicals

Growth occurs in response to certain chemical substances.

11 / QUICK COMPARISON

NERVOUS VS HORMONAL.

⚡
NERVOUS CONTROL

Fast Response

Uses electrical impulses through neurons. Responses are generally very fast and short-lived.

🧪
HORMONAL CONTROL

Chemical Response

Uses hormones carried through blood. Responses are generally slower and may last longer.
