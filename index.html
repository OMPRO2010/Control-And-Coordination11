01 • INTRODUCTION

CONTROL & COORDINATION

Control and coordination means controlling different activities of the body and coordinating them so that the body can respond properly to changes in its surroundings.

01

Stimulus

A stimulus is a change in the environment that causes a response.

02

Receptor

Receptors detect changes such as light, heat, sound, pressure and chemicals.

03

Response

A response is the reaction of an organism to a stimulus.

02 • NERVOUS SYSTEM

The nervous system helps organisms receive information, process it and produce a suitable response.

03 • COMMAND CENTRE

THE HUMAN BRAIN

The brain is the main coordinating centre of the human nervous system. It receives information, processes it and sends instructions to the body.

Brain Structure

CEREBRUM
CEREBELLUM
MEDULLA

Major Brain Parts

Cerebrum

The largest part of the brain. It controls thinking, memory, intelligence, reasoning, learning and voluntary actions.

Cerebellum

Maintains posture and balance and coordinates muscular movements.

Medulla

Controls involuntary actions such as breathing, heartbeat, swallowing and blood pressure.

Hypothalamus

Helps regulate several body functions and links the nervous system with hormonal control.

A neuron is the structural and functional unit of the nervous system. It carries electrical impulses from one part of the body to another.

Axon

Synapse

The small gap between neurons across which signals are transmitted.

Auxin

07 • PLANT RESPONSES

PLANT HORMONES & TROPISMS

Plants do not have a nervous system like animals. They respond to stimuli using chemical coordination and growth movements.

🌱
☀️

Phototropism

🌱
↓↓↓

Geotropism / Gravitropism

Phototropism

Growth of a plant part in response to light. Shoots generally grow towards light.

Geotropism

Growth response to gravity. Roots generally show positive geotropism.

Hydrotropism

Growth response towards water.

Chemotropism

Growth response to a chemical stimulus.

Thigmotropism

Growth response to touch, such as tendrils wrapping around support.

NERVOUS VS HORMONAL CONTROL

NERVOUS CONTROL

⚡ Uses electrical impulses

⚡ Very fast

⚡ Usually short-lived

⚡ Travels through neurons

HORMONAL CONTROL

🧪 Uses chemical messengers

🧪 Generally slower

🧪 Effects may last longer

🧪 Travels through blood
